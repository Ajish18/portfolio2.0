How an ERPNext module actually works

Two pieces of real work: the approval chain behind ETEMS, and the hook that keeps stock honest at Quickfix.

The 3-tier workflow behind ETEMS — an Expense Claim moves through role-gated states, each transition firing server-side validation before the next actor ever sees it.

DraftEmployee
Manager ReviewReports To
Finance CheckAccounts Manager
ReimbursedPayment Entry
  • Role gating — each transition is bound to a Workflow Action and Role, not a checkbox.
  • Server-side validation — advance limits, policy caps and duplicate claims rejected before submit.
  • Audit trail — every state change is versioned, giving 100% traceable financial compliance.
  • Auto Payment Entry — reimbursement posts against the employee's payable account on approval.

A trimmed version of the hook that deducts spare parts the moment a Job Card is completed.
